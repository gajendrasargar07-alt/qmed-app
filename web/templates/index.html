    About QMed

    QMed reads lab and pathology reports and estimates how closely the values match patterns linked with five common cancers: breast, lung, prostate, colon and cervical.

    How it works

    1. It reads the text from your file, including scans and photos.
    2. It checks that the file is a medical report and works out which type.
    3. Six models score the values, four classical and two quantum.
    4. The scores are combined into one result with a plain-language summary.

    Keep in mind

    • Results are estimates, not a diagnosis. Only a doctor can diagnose.
    • A low score does not rule anything out.
    • The breast model was trained on a public clinical dataset. The lung, prostate, colon and cervical models were trained on simulated data built from published clinical ranges, so treat those results with extra caution.
